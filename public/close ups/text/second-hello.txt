## Bittersweet Closeup #1: SECOND HELLO
my album, Bittersweet, finally released yesterday, & i've been wanting to spend some time talking about each individual song!!

so im gonna start doing some daily threads goin thru the tracklist in order, starting with the intro, **Second Hello** ❤️💚🧵⬇️

the bandcamp link, for y'all to listen along!! its the intro track, though, so i might have more to say about it than its runtime allows for lol

—

Second Hello's first moments establish the leitmotif of the entire album - originally a floaty lil instrumental i called '*kalia's theme*', bits and pieces of it just kept showing up in everything i was writing!! its pretty out there compared to some stuff on the album, but -

it shows up all over the place, from little melodic flourishes in Gentle Heart and Ballroom to very deliberate quotes in Paisley Patterns and Greatsword!! and as a melody, almost every song evokes bits and pieces of its arc

especially with the arrangement it gets here though, it really feels like a summary of the entire album to me!

and then we launch into... a remix of the title track from Autumn Every Day. which admittedly is a lil weird

i definitely waffled for a while over whether or not i should open my new album with such a brazen callback, but really i think it serves as a perfect reminder, to the world and to myself:

i've changed - a lot - but in the end, i'm still me

...but its also a remix of an older song of mine, so i don't have much technical to say about it >>;;

i did play the guitar on it tho! im no good at it, but any guitar stuff on Bittersweet that isn't credited to other people was recorded myself. which im real proud of actually!!

—

that's all i've got for today, but tomorrow i'll be back to talk about a personal favorite, Gentle Heart!!

keep an eye out, and check out the rest of Bittersweet if you haven't already ❤️💚❤️💚
