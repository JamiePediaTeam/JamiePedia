gummyworm is... a song that was very cathartic to write. i'm not comfortable going too far into the details of Why, but i will say it's pulling from personal experience.

abuse can destroy a person in the smallest of ways, but what's stuck with me the longest has been Trust.

to be gaslit, to be isolated in your pain, to have inhuman treatment juxtaposed with brief glimpses of some fake kindness... you forget how to trust *anyone*.

it makes me think of a sugar high. little pulses of energy that very quickly turn into a miserable, soul-sucking crash.

gummyworm is a song that, in isolation as a single or without the proper context, i would never have been okay with releasing. it is bleak. the most hope it expresses is the quietest little bitterness in the chorus; 'do i make you squirm?' 'does the memory hurt?' 'does this stay with you like it stays with me?'

my hope was, in the context of the entire album, i could show what healing from abuse can look like - to love yourself, *trust* yourself, and to find joy in trusting others.

and i'm really, really glad ppl like this song so much!

—

the synth refrain was written by [blackwinterwells](https://x.com/blackwntrwells) back in 2018, & it's probably my favorite part of this song musically. a melancholy melody, played loud & boisterously, as if to own it & accept the person you are Now. i'm real fond of it

i also used tons of izotope's fantastic vocalsynth plugin to add some texture to my voice throughout the song. i wanted to sound like a malfunctioning robot - coupled with some glitchy melodyne stuff, i think it does a good job of coming off as such

—
