some years ago, when i was really struggling emotionally, my gf honor gave me some really special words of comfort. it *all* meant so much to me, but there was one thing that really Stuck with me, a phrase lodged in my head

*"you are blessed with a mind capable of fire"*

she'd tell me later she said that b/c my star chart is like, 100% fire signs. which i think explains a lot. but it really got stuck in my head as this succinct summary of my relationship with Creativity - and as some comfort during a bout of severe burnout

i knew for ages that i wanted to turn it into some lyrics, but i didn't have enough of a musical Hook until way later, when i started working on Greatsword! my original sketch was just the chorus vocals and synths (and for a bit i genuinely thought it'd be a hyperpop-ass song)

and i had written that first chorus by itself as a counter to my previous Knightly type songs, in acceptance that... it was okay to Be Protected sometimes. to not always be the shield and the martyr. and this idea of the Sword as a bold, sharp, yet fragile & dangerous thing

the idea i had of Fire ended up fusing with this chorus into a wild mixed metaphor thing that forms the lyrical basis of Greatsword - fire to temper steel, fire to provide warmth, fire to Protect. and i as the sword, delicately tempered, a Voice born of passion

idk its hard to explain it in the confines of a twitter thread but its really just a song about passion!! and how it flourishes in moderation with deliberate love and care. a lesson that taking a massive break from music really taught me!

—

i wasn't sure what to do with the verses for a long time, lyrically or instrumentally, but while toying around with ideas i stumbled into something really goddamn cool from resampling the chorus loop & chopping the shit out of it. it was INCREDIBLY exciting when it clicked

(even though it was a little bit of a nightmare to work on in logic, as evidenced by the name of the track stack) 
[07 Greatsword Love as Fire - Image.png]

honestly, a lot of the music that i love sounds a lot more like this song than anything else ive made. i really like upbeat break shit! and it just made me so giddy to accidentally stumble backwards into making that same type of stuff and to love what i was hearing

lastly... this is the most overt quoting of kalia's theme that shows up outside of Second Hello, and that might be because this song is secretly also a little bit about another OC of mine hoo hoo hee hee i actually just write music about catgirls with massive swords
