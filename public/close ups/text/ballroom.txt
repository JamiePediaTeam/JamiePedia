## Bittersweet Closeup #9: BALLROOM
good morning once again!! next up in my threads about my album, Bittersweet, we've got **Ballroom**, featuring the one and only [Marcy Nabors](https://shadolith.neocities.org/)! i'm real sentimental about how this one came to be, so let me bear my heart a lil ❤️💚🧵⬇️

back in 2016, me and marcy decided to take a road trip together - we both were able and otherwise unoccupied at Just the right time to make it happen. we set out with the goal to make an album out of it, since many of the people we were visiting were also musicians

that album hasn't manifested yet, though we both still want to finish it! but that's beside the point.

there was one day, in the middle of it all, when we were staying in chicago w/ [rj](https://bsky.app/profile/rj.dcellgames.com) & [rachel lake](https://bsky.app/profile/rachel.dcellgames.com), in the city i would later end up living in myself years later

i don't remember Why, but i ended up feeling like shit that day and staying behind while the others were out n about, and i got to play around a bit with rj's moog. which like, if nothing else, was just really cool to be able to do like its a fucking Moog

i hadn't tweaked anything about the patch that was dialed in; i just got it running into logic and started noodling around. and this was the first thing i played! 
[09 Ballroom Close-Up - Video 2.mp4]

later on, when it was about time for me to head back home, marcy would help me flesh this idea out into a more fully-realized demo. and the melody just... stuck in the back of my mind, for all those years, always being something i'd hum to myself every so often

—

i tried re-approaching it a couple times before, but nothing stuck quite right. plus i wanted to hold onto it for the road trip album. but... idk. when it was time to work on bittersweet, i came back to it, feeling like a lifetime had passed since i first played its melody

and the words just started flowing! i got marcy to help with fleshing out the instrumentation & arranging some string parts, and all those years of that melody ringing in my ears culminated in something that i can confidently say is my favorite thing that i have ever written

a song about self-esteem, impostor syndrome, anxiety; about disparate parts coming together to form a whole self; about the paradox of honesty through performance; about being okay with just existing, as yourself or otherwise, and letting go of worry, even if just for a bit.

finding truth through the unity of two opposing ideas. the in-between of truth and fiction, artifice exalted to give voices to the voiceless...

when two come together as one. more than the sum of their parts. like dancers on the ballroom floor.

or, y'know. bitter and sweet

—

when marcy started working on the string arrangements, we settled pretty quickly on using some brazen Roland Sound Canvas sounds for them, and i think she did an absolutely fantastic job with fitting them in!! and blending them with more realistic strings throughout

it feels very much in concert w/ the theme of Ballroom, being this combination of the 'real' and 'fake' coming together to reach heights they could never achieve alone. plus it inspired me to tweet "jrpg stands for Jamie Really Paige Good" while we finished up the song

one last thing!! a lot of people have told me this song in particular means a lot to them, and i just want to sincerely say; thank you so much. i'm glad Any of this means something to Any of y'all, but... this song has been near and dear to me for years. it's special to me!

so, from the bottom of my heart, thank you all. ❤️💚

—
