i have a confession to make. its probably obvious with the lyrical content of this song, though.

paisley patterns is about an oc of mine. her name is paisley pudge. she was originally my d&d character in the campaign [reverieque](https://reverieque.carrd.co/) dms, but she also became my ffxiv character.

this is paisley pudge. thank you

pp4.jpg
anyways before i run all of my followers off by mmo posting let me explain myself!!!

i have always lowkey(?) been an RP nerd. whether ttrpgs, tumblr mfrp, ffxiv in-game rp, or longform discord paragraph type rp, it's always something i've loved & found a lot of joy in!

bittersweet's overarching Theme as an album is Creativity. its joys. its pains. how much unhealthy creativity can hurt you, how much fulfillment you can find in healthy creativity.

and getting back into rp during my break from music helped me learn how to create healthily again!

i really learned how much better it felt, how much better it made me as a person when Being A Creative wasn't some fundamental basis for my identity. and i made dear friends, ppl i wouldn't trade the world for!! i mean, shit, i know Que *because* of tumblr rp, for one

and paisley, the character, is the main persona that i bore through it all. shes become something of an icon for that newfound love of creativity! she means a lot to me!!! it felt dishonest *not* to commemorate her and everything else this silly rp stuff has brought me

so lyrically, this song sums up her backstory, but it's also just about... creative drive. and the joy of sharing it.

also its got a entire verse and chorus referencing [involutegirl](https://itch.io/profile/involutegirl)'s character Ekho Paialthe b/c she's been my longest-standing rp partner & lore co-developer lmao

musically, i actually went pretty low on the amount of autotune i used. partially this was just b/c i thought i sounded better Without it for once, and partially i was thinking abt the couple of other times i've put out songs w/o autotune where people really liked my voice...

there's also an entire CHORUS of people screaming "paisley pudge" at the end and i would not be able to fit everyone into one tweet but i will specifically thank [Samm Neiland](https://sammneiland.gay/), [Solatrus](https://solatrus.bandcamp.com), [monster_potion](https://crowsdanger.itch.io/), [internetFionna](https://bsky.app/profile/tautology.systems)  & my gf honor for lending their passionate pudge proclamations

lastly, all the assorted guitar stuff on this song was once again recorded by yours truly!! including the decidedly mediocre guitar solo. but its loud and gay and there's nobody louder and gayer than the one, the only, the immaculate and irrepressible.....
