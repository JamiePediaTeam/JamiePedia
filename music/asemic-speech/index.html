Bittersweet Breakdown #3: ASEMIC SPEECH

its a pleasantly warm day in mid-july, 2019. i am in wisconsin, in a cabin in the woods w/ my friend rocky. i'm resolved to at least flex Some creative muscles during my break from retail-working city-living nightmare time.

and then i spot this tweet
[03 Asemic Speech - QRT HYPERLIGHT.png]

my friend bluffy (who would later go on to sing on the track Close!!) had tweeted an oddly poetic phrase that quickly latched onto my brain. some days prior, i was entertaining a lil wikipedia-induced fixation on the concept of asemic writing - writing without semantic meaning

the pieces starting rapidly coming together, and i ran to convert a closet into a makeshift vocal booth so i could record my first demo of Asemic Speech's chorus! all serendipitously inspired by some flippant poetic shitpost by a friend. which i suppose is fitting

—

in terms of structure and harmony, i was 100% just trying to imitate one of my favorite bands, The Beths↗. tight power pop harmonies, leaning on that bVII chord, playful call & response; it all feels to me just like how songs w/ this theme are Supposed to sound!

i also wrote it in a different way than usual; normally, i write songs by starting with the chorus & going through everything else In Order, but i had actually written the bridge before even having a solid verse down! that... led to some pretty long verses/choruses tho lmfao

AND THEN WE GET TO THE ABSOLUTELY FUCKIN BULLSHIT BONKERS GUITAR SOLO FROM TELEBASHER GOD ITS SO GOOOOOOOOOD

i literally just tweeted out asking for someone who could, in my words, 'absolutely fucking shred', and i got pointed her way. and i'm so fuckin glad abt it!!!!

my instructions were to write something that sounded like a pop punk guitarist melting while playing a jazz solo and i think that was captured perfectly lmao. sincerely blew me out of the water!! please listen to her music its all so incredibly good
