Bittersweet Breakdown #10: BITTERSWEET

we're nearing the end of the daily threads for my album, Bittersweet, and that means it's time to talk about the title track!! an ode to creativity itself, its highs and lows - and like any other relationship, how dependency on it can destroy you. ❤️💚🧵⬇️

i've been thinking a lot recently about the long period of burnout i went through, starting back in late 2017. between bittersweet's release, the live set i did for Digital Stars, and my continued work on other projects, happening in the spaces Between my life instead of Being it

it's... put things into perspective, in a major way. i used to think i had to force myself to work on music Constantly, that every little doodle had to become a full song, always thinking in terms of projects before a single note had been written down

it's obvious in hindsight that i was putting my creative process first, but in my mind, it was simply what i had always done - create. but unchecked, it became this festering, miserable relationship that threatened to consume me, that turned me into the worst version of myself

i had to break it off! i moved to chicago in late 2018, and fresh off the heels of a live show i had thoroughly bombed, it ended up being easy to just... step away. still opening logic up every so often for little doodles, but i mostly just... did other things? and it was good!!

much of this album is about what i learned from that; finding other things i love, learning to exist at peace alone. reckoning with the parts of myself i left to decay.

but this song is about how, in spite of everything, i still love creativity. it just had to be Different.

—

i wrote the first demo of this back in late 2019, just grabbing hold of the first couple lines of the chorus. as i worked more on it, though, it became clear that it was really such a succinct summary of the story i was trying to tell! finding balance, nuance, peace

i honestly don't have much else to say other than this; i'm really glad it's the title track!!

my one note on the production side is that my roommate called the last chorus the gayest-sounding thing i had ever written. and yeah. it really is

—

that's it for now! tomorrow, i'll be wrapping these threads up by talking about the endlessly cathartic Close, featuring Bluffy and Mattmatatt!!
