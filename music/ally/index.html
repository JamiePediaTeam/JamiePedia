Bittersweet Breakdown #8: ALLY

next up for my threads about my album, Bittersweet, we've got the bright, loud, joyous Ally!! which, for anyone just tuning in, is one of the songs i played last night↗ at #DigitalStarsMIKUGUMI!! time to talk about the most Jamie Paige song possible ❤️💚🧵⬇️
[08 Ally Close-Up - Video 1.mp4]

as always, here's the song on bandcamp so you can listen along/check out the full album!

Ally is one of the last songs i finished for bittersweet, and there was part of me convinced i needed to make it a Soft or Quiet song. of course it would be 'jamie paige' soft, which in my mind meant a full 80's-ass ballad complete with very straightforward drums

and that's how i originally wrote the chorus!! but the problems were. one, it wasn't super inspiring, and two, it sounded NOTHING like anything else on bittersweet.

then i literally just replaced the slow gated drums with a breakbeat w/o changing anything else & it was perfect

when first sketching the chorus, I also thought back to some really old demos, and found this one i had thrown together less than a month after Autumn Every Day came out... which i figure it's safe to say is pretty obviously the basis of Ally ^^
[08 Ally Close-Up - Video 2.mp4]

somehow, on an album with a song about my ffxiv character and everything, this feels like the most self-indulgent song to me! the choppy saw synths, loud arps, vocaloid duets, and the corny but sincere lyricism...

—

i don't think this song is as musically adventurous as some of the other things on Bittersweet? but i'm super proud of it - lyrically and sonically, it's so perfectly Something I Would Make, and i've found there's a hell of a lot of merit in making music like that!!

and lyrically, yeah, it's a typical song about companionship. but my subject, the person i was singing to? was myself!

there's an idea that's come up a lot for me when approaching mental health issues, the idea of Being Your Own Friend, and i didn't really get it for a long time

but i've found that treating yourself like the product of Companionship, thinking of the self in terms of parts that can Work Together... for me, that's been really healing in a lot of ways! especially as someone that's struggled immensely internally for a long time

so, Ally is a sort of. self-love song, i suppose? that's what it means to me, at least. and i've talked before abt using GUMI for expressing myself in ways my own voice couldn't... on this song, and really this Album, its like she became the voice of another part of myself?

i simply think that's neat!! and its a theme explored more in tomorrow's song, Ballroom❤️💚

a-also one more thing; the song ends with a brazen usage of the old Logic plugin "AURogerBeep", at the suggestion of Marcy Nabors. all it does is makes beeps after a set amount of silence

—

anyways, that's about it! tomorrow, we'll be discussing the deeply sentimental Ballroom, featuring marcy herself, and how much its melody means to me!!

thanks for reading!! check out Bittersweet here if you haven't already ❤️💚
