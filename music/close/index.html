Bittersweet Breakdown #11: CLOSE

good morning! it's time for the last of the daily threads about my album, Bittersweet, and what better way to send em off than by talking about Close, featuring the delightful Bluffy and Mattmatatt? ❤️💚🧵⬇️

as always, here's the song on bandcamp so you can listen along/check out the full album!!

this track laid unfinished in my project files for ages, since back before i even tried to name & sort things properly, so its demo is aptly titled "dumbgayshit.logicx". all i had were the bg vocals for the chorus - urgently asking, "can i still be close to you?"

it laid untouched for ages, but it was actually one of the few songs i made significant progress on back in 2019, while taking a break from music-making - i'd say about 65-70% of the song was in its finished form by the time i set it aside again!! and that wouldn't have happened if not for the incredible, impeccable help i received from bluffy!! bluffy is an absolute legend and a very dear friend of mine, and also to my knowledge doesn't really do music? but bluffy absolutely Killed it - i actually wrote my verse second, based off bluffy's melody

—

the image i always had in my head for this song was something like peter gabriel's live performance of Come Talk to Me, from the secret world film. it's such a simple image, yet endlessly dramatic and evocative - wrestling with the confines of Distance↗
<iframe width="400" height="300" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/JRLjpXLEp1A" title="Peter Gabriel - Come Talk To Me" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Close was me taking this idea and transferring it to the digital! the song bounces between mono, low bitrate verses and wide, high fidelity choruses, long-distance yearning and love becoming physical and Real, back and forth - a feeling i've been familiar with for a long time

—

the bridge was written just a few months ago, while tying all these tracks together into an album; it was written as a reassurance, to myself, to my friends, to my family, to everyone i love, that it would be okay; that we would be together again soon

and especially as i find myself at the close of a blissful few days getting to be with my girlfriend in real, physical space again after almost 2 years... there aren't really words. i'm glad i kept my trust.

anyways then there's a fucking SICK guitar solo from matt

the alt takes i got to hear were wild - at least one sounded like some green grass and high tides shit - but we settled on something real nice in the end, i think!! it also quotes bizarre love triangle at the beginning. that was matt's idea. its delightful

—

one last thing; the final lyrics. they were actually the words i had originally written the chorus melody to, after flippantly singing that phrase to myself on a discord call ages ago. and i honestly don't remember who, but somebody insisted i genuinely use those as the lyrics

i'm really glad i did. because i've been through a lot. i've had a hell of a time, with myself, with others, with my art. but at the other end of it all?

yeah! jamie paige! that's my fucking name!!

in this moment... i am real. i am okay.

and i will live another day!

—

thank you for reading these threads, for listening to this album, for letting me know this music means something to you all. i am so unbelievably goddamn glad.

have a good one, y'all!! take care💚❤️
