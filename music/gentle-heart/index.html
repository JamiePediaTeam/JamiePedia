Bittersweet Breakdown #2: GENTLE HEART

hey there!! it's time for another song explaining thread for my album, Bittersweet, and this time it's for the song Gentle Heart!

this song's got a storied history, and it gives me a chance to talk a little abt the album i almost made instead... ❤️💚🧵⬇️
[02 Gentle Heart Close-Up - Video 1.mp4]
here's the link to the full song on bandcamp if you wanna listen along!!

Gentle Heart was the first song i finished writing for Bittersweet, back in january of 2018 or so, and other than polish work, guitar tracking (again, by yours truly >:3c), and some really detailed vocal editing, it was basically in its finished form back then! i was tempted to release it as a single at a couple points, but back then i didn't like releasing singles unless there was a full release i could tie them to fairly soon after. so, i decided - it would be the blueprint for the rest of the album! jamie paige Sad And Dark Time!

i settled on the name Ambivalence, and intended for it to be the last release i put out w/ the letter A (in my mission to alphabetize my entire discography, of course). soon after, i wrote Gummyworm, too! and then... well, there's a reason that album didn't happen

i will likely release the other demos i wrote around that time soon-ish, but they're Rough, and writing about the life shit you've struggled with while you're going through it is only so sustainable. i was also trying to brute force my way through newfound burnout post-Autumn

inevitably, work on it stalled, and fairly soon after, all work on music stalled too. i'll go into more detail on how that played out when i talk about the song Bittersweet, but for now, that meant Ambivalence was shelved.

which is good imo! it wouldn't've been a good album lol

—

anyways, the song itself!! it was originally envisioned as a VERY conventional jamie paige song, arranged sorta like Ally, but as the lyrics got moodier, so did the instrumental. you can really still hear it when the drums are taken out, i think
[02 Gentle Heart Close-Up - Video 2.mp4]
but the drums are where a lot of the song's energy are! some of yall know i used to make breakcore & other real loud, noisy sorts of music, and Gentle Heart was my attempt to bring that knowledge into what i'm doing now. just some unabashed loud choppy amen break shit

i went super simple lyrically to contrast with the incredibly busy instrumental, and threw in interest mostly with tons of vocal microedits - i'd show off the absurd amounts of automation/tracks but that wld require screenshotting one of my nightmare project files ^^;;

either way, i think it all makes for a song w/ tons of contrast!! clashing elements coming together in a delightful hyperspeed medley. an oxymoron of a song.

i guess you could say.....

its *bittersweet*
