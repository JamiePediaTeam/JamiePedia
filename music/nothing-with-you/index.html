well the gist of this song is pretty easy to sum up - i hate rainbow capitalism! i hate the idea of trying to fit queerness into some pristine flawless easily-digested bubble for society at large to consume! im aware of the irony of writing a catchy sub-4 minute pop song abt this

but like, let me clarify: i love pride!! but not like Big Parade With Corporate Sponsors And Cops, Rainbow Brand Twitter Avatar type shit. like, unity and family and Love, unabashedly queer! thats how i try to live my Life!!

anyways, i started writing this song during pride month; i was heading into the chicago loop for my Day Job, passing by tons of rainbow advertisements and empty platitudes plastered everywhere. feeling disgustingly Unseen. and i just remember thinking to myself;

"if this is what it means to be Seen, to be Accepted, to be Something, i would much rather just be nothing"

that idea quickly latched onto my brain, and i very quickly went and started writing a bunch of words that would end up becoming Nothing (with You)!

—

musically, a lot of little obsessions of mine get to show up: straight-up general midi soundfonts, mp3 compression, weird text-to-speech vocals - the idea of having them in tandem with the vocals was from a real old demo of mine, refined in this iteration

the mp3 compression stuff was thanks to goodhertz's Lossy↗, which is just a FANTASTIC plugin. musical mp3 compression emulator! its the coolest thing!!

i also decided super quickly that it was going to be sung by gumi

she does straight & steady stuff really well i think. and not only did it turn out really well, but i got hit with inspiration and ended up filling this album with a bunch more vocaloid shit! not as much as i would've liked but... i've got plans!! i'll say that much

lastly, the midi outro and the lil pan flute solo thing were both ideas my roommate joly helped me come up with! and i think they give the song a much-needed extra lil kick of playfulness

—

anyways i think that's it! tomorrow i get to be TREMENOUSLY on my bullshit and tell you all about paisley pudge when i talk about Paisley Patterns!!! thanks for reading! and give Bittersweet a listen if you haven't already ❤️💚
