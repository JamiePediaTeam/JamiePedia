~ Chapter 1: But Only Truly Backwards ~

There's a site I lurk and very rarely post on known as Battle of the Bits↗.

The general premise is that people can host Battles, music-making challenges designed to be completed within a set timeframe, most often only one hour, with restrictions both in format and theme. It’s very much chiptune/tracker music centric, but there are formats that are inclusive to all types of gear - notably, the format “allgear”! These are the battles my scant few contributions to the site have mostly ended up in, and while I’ve never been a regular, I do genuinely credit doing OHBs with making me a better musician.

This was mostly just a couple week stint back in 2018, though, and many factors have kept me from participating more often than just a couple luckily-timed battles here and there - not to mention I took a two-year break from seriously making music at all also starting in 2018. However, one fateful December day last year, I was feeling nostalgic and full of creative energy, and loaded the site back up hoping to find a challenge to contribute to -

Only to discover that there was a whole extended series of autumnal allgear challenges that had wrapped up mere days prior. They had a cutesy name and everything! FALLGEAR! I was aghast! That’s my shtick! I’m the autumn bitch!!

Dejected. Despondent. Defeated and desolate. The fates had dangled their red string in front of my face, a pane of glass between us, and I had just smacked into it with such raucous and foolish force I’d broken my nose.

This wouldn’t stand. Thus began my own One-Hour Battle against the shifting sands of time itself. It ultimately took two hours, but thankfully, an old and constant companion of mine found me in my hour of need.

~ Chapter 2: All I Wanna Do ~

These manic bursts of creative energy have historically always been how I’ve made my music, and to some extent, they still are. These self-imposed time limit challenges have mostly taught me how to make the most of them, and how to summon that energy at will instead of it simply manifesting at its own whim.

Even so, it is incredibly difficult to explain the struggle I have with executive function.

I can think to do something. I can feel the impulse welling up inside me. I can push and pull at my body, trying to fire the appropriate neurons. I can scream bloody murder at myself. I can cry my damn eyes out. Sometimes, none of this is enough to make my body move.

If it was a matter of desire, it would be easy, but wanting something simply isn’t enough. There is a great, wide gap between me and the things I want to do, the words I want to say, the people I want to love, and I’ve built my bridges and flying machines and telephone wires to make that gap smaller but on my worst days all the therapy and mindfulness and medication and agony and longing in the world just isn’t enough.

If you also have ADHD, this all might sound familiar to you.

It feels a lot like heartbreak.

I’ve made my love of the band Prefab Sprout known publicly many times over at this point. Paddy McAloon is maybe my favorite songwriter of all time, and for good reason - I struggle to think of anyone else who can pen such mellifluous and heart-wrenching words with such effortless precision.

~ Chapter 4: A Fire Lit Within My Chest ~

iwticf (chorus layers)

[iwticf (chorus layers).mp3]

Longing is a central theme of this album - the album motif is a thesis statement, really! The conflict between what currently Is and what you Want is at the core of basically every song, and IWTICF leans so heavily on said leitmotif because, in my opinion, it is the one song that sums up Constant Companions as a whole the best.

I had originally intended for there to be a secondary motif alongside the melody, in the form of saxophone. I’ve always been a very big fan of how sax sounds, and the only reason I didn’t play saxophone in middle school band was because my parents didn’t want to deal with hearing me practice sax 24/7. That being said, they instead let me become a percussionist, which in hindsight makes absolutely zero sense if noise was really their concern so I don’t know what was up with that.

Ultimately, the sax motif idea didn’t really pan out, but it still exists very prominently in both IWTICF and Cadmium Colors. Whenever I manage to finally properly pre-plan one of my albums before I start working on it, I swear, I’ll be unstoppable.

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K3Z-7lezplU]

I Wish That I Could Fall is also one of two MVs for the album that were filmed and edited by yours truly! I wanted both IWTICF and Machine Love to have a similar visual style, and the disconnect between the artificial (computer screens, projections) and the tangible (live-action footage) very much felt appropriate for both. There’s an inherent longing to me, being on one side of an impassable pane of glass and trying to find connection with what’s on the other side.

That being said, it was a pain in the ass to film and an even worse pain in the ass to edit. For some godforsaken reason, I decided to use exclusively Final Cut Pro for all my motion graphics work that would’ve been far better suited to something like After Effects or aviutil or even Apple Motion, for god’s sake. The project files for these are all hundreds of gigabytes large. Please don’t be like me.

Anyways, it’s maybe a silly point of pride, but I would like to point out - There is no stock footage used in either Machine Love or IWTICF!! Every single bit of live-action footage seen in both MVs was filmed either by me or by someone I directly asked to film it. For years now, I’ve been filming random moments of travel or motion, and I finally found a use for a bunch of it!

…My next video will hopefully be smaller in scale.
